Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. The short version: your names never leave your device.

1. What we collect

FFNames.pro is a static tool. We do not require accounts, and we do not collect names, email addresses or personal data through the generator. Names you type and names you favorite are processed and stored entirely in your browser (localStorage) and are never transmitted to us.

2. Local storage

Your favorites list is saved in your browser's local storage under the key ff_favs. Clearing your browser data removes it. You can also remove it anytime with the "Clear all" button in the Favorites dialog.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Fonts - loaded from Google's CDN to render the site typography; Google may receive your IP address as part of any web request. See Google's own privacy policy for details.
  • Hosting / CDN - like any website, our host may keep standard server logs (IP, user agent, timestamps) for security and abuse prevention.

4. Cookies

We do not set tracking cookies. The site runs without analytics trackers by default; if analytics are ever added, this policy will be updated first.

5. Children

Free Fire is rated for teens and above. We knowingly collect no personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Your rights

Since we do not maintain personal data stores, there is nothing to export or delete on our side - everything lives in your browser. Questions? Contact contact@ffnames.pro.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
