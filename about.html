About FFNames.pro

A free, fan-made toolbox for Free Fire nicknames.

What is this site?

FFNames.pro is a Free Fire name generator and symbols library. Type any nickname and instantly preview it in 54 unicode styles - fonts like Old English, Cursive and Bubble, plus iconic FF frames like ꧁꧂ wraps. Browse 860+ curated names across 28 categories, or copy any of 560+ symbols one tap at a time.

Why we built it

Every player wants a nickname that stands out in the kill-feed, but making one by hand is slow and most copy-paste sites are cluttered and full of broken symbols. FFNames.pro keeps it simple: clean interface, mobile-first design, verified characters, accurate character counts against the 12-character limit, and honest guides about rename costs and rules.

Everything is free

  • No account, no login, no paywall
  • Unlimited generations and copies
  • Favorites saved locally in your browser - nothing leaves your device
  • No watermark on any name
