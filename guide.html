How to Change Your Free Fire Name

The complete 2026 guide - rename steps, costs, the 12-character rule, guild renames and nickname safety.

Quick answer

Tap your avatar in the top-left of the lobby, tap the pencil icon next to your nickname, paste your new stylish name, and confirm. Your first rename is free; after that it costs a Name Change Card or 390 diamonds. Nicknames are limited to 12 characters, symbols included.

Step-by-step: rename your nickname

  1. Copy your new name from the generator or any collection on this site.
  2. Open Free Fire (or Free Fire MAX) and wait for the lobby to load.
  3. Tap your avatar in the top-left corner to open your profile card.
  4. Tap the pencil / edit icon next to your current nickname. If you only see the settings gear, back out - the pencil is on the profile card itself.
  5. Clear the old name, long-press the field and paste the new one.
  6. Confirm. The game spends a Name Change Card if available, otherwise it asks for 390 diamonds.
Tip: keep your base nickname 8-9 characters long. That leaves room for symbols, frames and a guild tag like [FF] or [OP] without hitting the 12-character cap.

What a rename costs

  • First change - free. New accounts get one complimentary rename, so make it count.
  • Name Change Card. Single-use item. Full price in the store, much cheaper in the Guild Store (a small diamond amount plus 200 guild tokens), and sometimes free from events and redeem codes.
  • 390 diamonds. The direct price when you have no card.

The 12-character rule

Free Fire counts every character toward the limit, including spaces and unicode symbols. That is why the styled names on this site use compact frames. Every name card here shows its exact character count and warns you when a name goes over 12 characters.

Changing your guild name

  1. Open the Guild panel from the lobby.
  2. Tap Settings / Edit next to the guild name (Guild Leader only).
  3. Type the new name and confirm - it costs 500 gold, not diamonds.

Need inspiration? Browse the Guild & Clan collection for ready-made guild names.

Nickname rules - what gets you banned

Unicode symbols and styled letters are fine and used by millions of players. These are not:

  • Offensive words, slurs and hate speech
  • Religious or political mockery
  • Impersonating Garena staff, moderators or known personalities
  • Advertising and website links in nicknames
Warning: nicknames that break the rules can be reset by Garena without notice, and repeat offenses can lead to account bans. Keep it clean.

Blank / invisible names

Invisible names use the Hangul Filler character (U+3164) - a real but invisible unicode letter. Copy it from the symbols library, paste it 2-3 times into the rename box and confirm. Popular, but remember teammates can't search you by name.

Why symbols show as boxes

If a symbol looks like an empty square on your phone, your system font lacks that glyph. It will usually still render correctly inside Free Fire. Test with the classic set first - ꧁ ༒ ꧂ ツ ✦ - which render on virtually every device.

Checklist before you confirm

  • 12 characters or fewer?
  • No banned words?
  • Copied the exact styled version (not a partial selection)?
  • Have a Name Change Card or 390 diamonds ready?
