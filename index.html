How to Change Your Free Fire Name

Six quick steps - takes under two minutes once you have copied your new name.

1

Copy a name

Generate a style above or pick one from the collections, then tap its Copy button.

2

Open your profile

Launch Free Fire and tap your avatar in the top-left corner of the lobby.

3

Tap the pencil icon

Next to your current nickname, tap the small yellow pencil / edit icon.

4

Paste the new name

Clear the old name, long-press the field and choose Paste.

5

Confirm the change

The game uses a free Name Change Card if you have one, otherwise diamonds.

6

Show it off
